Principles of professional email writing

  • Use a clear subject line that explains the purpose of the email.
  • Address the recipient respectfully and use an appropriate greeting.
  • Keep your message focused, polite and easy to understand.
  • Use correct spelling, punctuation and professional language.
  • Check that the recipient's email address and any attachments are correct before sending.
  • Respond within a reasonable time and avoid repeated follow-ups in a short period.
  • Use a professional email address and include a suitable signature.
