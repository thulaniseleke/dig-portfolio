My learning approach

A practical routine for turning learning resources into knowledge and useful project experience.

Step 01

Understand the concept

Start with course notes and trusted learning materials. Focus on understanding the reason behind a concept rather than memorising steps.

Step 02

Practise with examples

Apply the concept through exercises, calculations, spreadsheet tasks, queries or small programming activities.

Step 03

Apply it to a project

Use the skills in practical work, such as cleaning a dataset, analysing variables, building charts or developing an interactive dashboard.

Step 04

Review and improve

Check the results, identify mistakes, document what was learned and revisit difficult concepts. Keep a record of progress and areas to develop.

Principles for effective learning

  • Set clear and achievable study goals.
  • Practise consistently instead of relying only on last-minute preparation.
  • Use exercises and real datasets to reinforce theoretical knowledge.
  • Ask questions and seek clarification when a concept is unclear.
  • Evaluate the reliability of information before using it in assignments.
  • Reference external sources and acknowledge other people's work.
  • Reflect on progress and identify the next skill to develop.

Resource note: The links above lead to external educational websites. Some resources may require an account or offer a mixture of free and paid content. Always follow your institution's requirements when using external materials in assignments.
