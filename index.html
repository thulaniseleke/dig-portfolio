A little about me

Curious by nature. Focused on growth.

My journey is centred on learning, developing practical skills and understanding how technology can be used to solve real-world problems. Studying Business Intelligence and Data Analytics gives me the opportunity to combine analytical thinking with digital tools and business knowledge.

I have explored data preparation, visualisation and dashboard development using Excel and Power BI. Working with a New York City taxi dataset introduced me to the process of cleaning data, examining different variable types, building charts and creating interactive KPI elements.

Outside my academic work, my experience in administration and day-to-day responsibilities has helped me appreciate communication, organisation, teamwork and attention to detail. I aim to keep building on these experiences as I progress in my studies and career.
