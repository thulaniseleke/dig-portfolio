Reflecting on my learning, the skills I am developing, the experiences that have shaped me, and the direction in which I want to grow.

My Learning Journey

My journey as a student of Business Intelligence and Data Analytics at Botswana Accountancy College is an opportunity to build knowledge that can be applied to real problems. I am particularly interested in the relationship between mathematics, computing and the interpretation of information. I want to understand not only how data is processed, but also how it can be presented in a way that supports better decisions.

My earlier education at Radikolo Junior Secondary School and Premix Academy helped establish my academic foundation. Mathematics and science contributed to my interest in logical reasoning and structured problem-solving. As I progress in my studies, I recognise that learning requires consistency, curiosity and a willingness to improve when a topic is difficult.

My experiences outside the classroom have also influenced how I approach responsibility. Assisting with flower orders, supporting football player administration and helping with agricultural payment administration have exposed me to different tasks that require organisation, communication and attention to detail. These responsibilities have helped me appreciate the importance of completing tasks carefully and keeping information organised.

Learning becomes more meaningful when knowledge can be connected to practical responsibilities.

Skills and Development

One important area of development for me is analytical thinking. My interest in mathematics provides a useful foundation for examining information logically, identifying patterns and considering how problems can be approached. Studying data analytics gives me an opportunity to strengthen these abilities through practical tools and structured analysis.

Working with a New York City taxi dataset has provided practical exposure to data preparation and visualisation. The work involved cleaning data, exploring quantitative and categorical variables, creating charts and developing interactive dashboard elements using Excel and Power BI. I also worked with measures relating to average fare, average tip amount, average trip distance, total revenue and total trips.

This type of work has shown me that analysis involves more than producing charts. The data needs to be prepared properly, the variables need to be understood, and the visual presentation needs to make the information easier to interpret. It has encouraged me to become more careful and systematic when working through analytical tasks.

Communication and teamwork are also important areas of development. Academic tasks and administrative responsibilities require people to share information, understand instructions and contribute reliably. I want to continue improving my ability to explain ideas clearly, cooperate with others and manage my time effectively.

Challenges and Lessons

Learning a new technical skill can require patience, especially when a task involves several steps or when the result does not immediately match expectations. My approach is to break a task into smaller parts, review the available information and work through the process systematically rather than rushing to a result.

I am learning that mistakes and uncertainty can be useful opportunities to identify gaps in my understanding. Asking questions, practising a procedure and reviewing the outcome can help me develop a stronger grasp of a subject. I also recognise the importance of taking responsibility for my own learning instead of depending entirely on instructions from others.

Another lesson is that professional development includes more than technical ability. Reliability, respect, communication and organisation matter when working with people and information. I want these qualities to remain part of the way I approach both academic work and future employment.

Future Development

Going forward, I want to deepen my understanding of data analysis tools, strengthen my ability to interpret datasets and improve how I present findings through clear visualisations. I also want to build confidence in communicating analytical ideas to people who may not have a technical background.

I plan to support my development through consistent practice, independent study, constructive feedback and participation in projects that allow me to apply what I learn. Keeping records of my work and reflecting on what went well or what needs improvement will help me track my progress over time.

Overall, my learning journey is still developing. I have a foundation on which to build, practical experiences that have introduced me to different responsibilities, and a clear interest in analytical work. My goal is to become a capable, adaptable and responsible professional who continues learning and uses knowledge constructively.

My development is an ongoing process of learning, applying, reflecting and improving.
